Testing Life in the UK

A free, ad-free study aid for the UK Home Office Life in the UK Test.

What it is. An iPhone and iPad app with 500+ chapter-grouped practice questions, full 24-question mock tests on the same pass mark as the real exam, a Review Pile that drills your wrong answers, and progress tracking by chapter. Everything stays on your device — no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Support

Found a wrong answer, a typo, or have a suggestion? Email testinglifeintheuk@gmail.com. Replies usually within a couple of days.

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your iOS version and device (Settings → General → About)
  • The app version (Settings tab in the app → Version)
